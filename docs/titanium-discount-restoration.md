# Titanium staff discount restoration

On 11 November 2026, the user requested restoration of Titanium's 20% discount.
The till's existing percentage toggle and pricing logic were intact, but the
central `prod-staff-discount` catalogue record was missing.

Restored the original record for `tenant-01`, named Staff 20% Discount, SKU
`SRV-STF-03`. It is active as a zero-price service used by the existing till
discount toggle, with no stock tracking and `showOnMenu: false`. No ordinary
product prices, membership records, staff permissions or other tenants changed.
The restoration is audited as `catalogue.staff_discount_restored`, reference
`TITANIUM-RESTORE-20-20261111`.

Validation: frontend production build and 11 pricing/tender tests passed. A
Chromium check read the restored live record and exercised the actual till
handlers: KES 1,000 becomes KES 800 with a KES 200 discount; toggling it off
returns the total to KES 1,000. Artesano has no corresponding discount record.
No sale, stock movement or payment was generated by verification.

Firebase Hosting deployment completed on 11 November 2026.
